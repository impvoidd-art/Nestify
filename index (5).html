Questions students ask

How fast will my kit arrive?

Most kits reach hostels and PGs in metro cities within 48 hours. Tell us your move-in date at checkout and we'll deliver the day before.

Can I return a kit if something's missing or wrong?

Yes. You have 7 days to return any unused item, and missing items are replaced or refunded, no questions asked.

How does renting from another student work?

Pick an item, send a request, and the student replies within a day. You pay the first month plus a refundable deposit, then collect the item from them or have it delivered.

Do I get my deposit back?

Yes. The deposit is refunded within 3 days of the item being returned in the same condition.

I'm leaving my PG. How do I earn from my stuff?

Open the List page, add a photo and a monthly price, and we handle requests, pickup slots and payouts to your UPI.

Leaving your PG? Lend it to the next student.

Fridges, tables, fans, mattresses. If you can't carry it home, someone nearby needs it.

How listing works

  1. Add a photo and a monthly price. We suggest a fair rent based on similar items nearby.
  2. Pick your handover dates. Students can collect from your PG or we arrange pickup.
  3. Approve a request. You choose who gets your item.
  4. Get paid. Rent goes to your UPI every month, and the deposit comes back to the renter at handover.
